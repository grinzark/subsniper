How it works & why it's safe: SubSniper only reads reddit.com pages you're already viewing in your own logged-in session — no Reddit API, no scraping, and no SubSniper server. Scoring runs entirely on your device. It never posts, comments, upvotes, or acts on your behalf — drafts are copy-paste only. The one exception to "stays on your device" is the optional AI draft below: if you add your own Anthropic key and click Generate with AI, that post's text is sent to Anthropic.
